# Styles: animation techniques (next batch)

Styles 22 to 33 come from classic animation techniques. Each one is a way of moving through a page, not a skin.

## Shipped: `flipbook`, `pop-up-book`, `pinscreen`, `sand`, `zoetrope`. Dropped: `rotoscope` (styles 22 to 27)

| Style | Technique | What moving through the page does |
|---|---|---|
| `pinscreen` | Alexeieff and Parker's pinscreen (1933) | A wall of pins whose shadows form the image; scroll pushes the pins into the next picture, the pointer presses them |
| `sand` | Sand animation (Caroline Leaf) | One continuous sand picture; your swipe or scroll brushes it into the next scene |
| `zoetrope` | Zoetrope, praxinoscope | A spinning drum; the faster you scroll, the more it comes alive, slow down and it breaks into frames |
| `flipbook` | Flipbook | You thumb the corner and the page flips through drawn frames |
| `pop-up-book` | Pop-up book | Each section is a spread; open it and the scene folds up out of the page |
| `rotoscope` | Rotoscoping (*A Scanner Darkly*, "Take On Me") | Film and images traced live into ink; real motion with a hand-drawn line |
